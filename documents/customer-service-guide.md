# Customer Service Guide

## Returns

Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery when the item is unused, undamaged, and includes the original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item is inspected.

## Shipping

Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days after the order is processed. Expedited shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days. Shipping times can vary during holidays or severe weather.

## Warranty

Most products include a one-year limited warranty for manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, normal wear, or unauthorized repairs.

## Troubleshooting

For device setup issues, ask the customer to confirm the device is powered on, connected to the correct network, and running the latest available firmware. If the issue continues after restart and firmware update, escalate to technical support.

## Escalation

Escalate cases involving safety concerns, repeated failed troubleshooting, billing disputes, warranty exceptions, or requests for private account changes that cannot be handled from public support documents.
